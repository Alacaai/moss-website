Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026 · Effective June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Moss handles information when you use the Moss app and website. Our principle is simple: collect as little as practical, keep data on your device when we can, and never sell your personal information.

Information We Collect

We may collect account information, plant data you add, support messages, and limited device or diagnostic information used to maintain and improve Moss.

Plant Photos

Moss is designed to keep plant photos on your device whenever possible. Photos are not uploaded unless you choose a feature that requires sharing, sync, backup, support, or a misidentification report.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict use of your personal information by emailing mossplant@gmail.com.
