Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026 · Effective June 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Moss handles information when you use the Moss app and website. Our principle is simple: collect as little as practical, keep data on your device when we can, tell you plainly when information leaves your device, and never sell your personal information.

Information We Collect

We may collect optional account information (email and name), plant data you add (including photos and in-app assistant messages), a user identifier, purchase history, crash diagnostics, and, only with your permission, coarse location used solely for weather-based care tips.

Plant Photos

When you identify or diagnose a plant, the photo you submit is sent to our servers and AI provider to generate your result. Other photos stay on your device unless you choose a feature that requires uploading them, such as sync or backup.

AI Processing

No AI runs in the app or on your device. All AI processing happens on our servers: the app calls our backend, which calls Anthropic's Claude API. Only the plant photo and your assistant questions are sent; your name, email, and account identifiers are not, and this data is not used to train AI models.

Your Rights

You can delete your account in the app, which removes your account and the associated data from our servers. Depending on where you live, you may also ask to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict use of your personal information by emailing mossplant@gmail.com.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: mossplant@gmail.com, Istanbul / Turkiye.
