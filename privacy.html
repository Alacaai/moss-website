Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026 · Effective April 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Moss Labs, Inc. ("Moss", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the Moss mobile application and the website at mossplant.com (together, the "Service"). Our guiding principle is simple: collect as little as possible, keep it on your device when we can, and never sell what we do collect.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information. If you create a Moss account, we collect your email address and, optionally, your name.
  • Plant data. Names, photos, care schedules, and notes you add about your plants.
  • Support messages. The contents of any email, form submission, or chat you send us.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data. Device model, operating system version, app version, crash logs, approximate region (derived from IP), and anonymized usage events (e.g. "scan started") used to improve the app.
  • Photos you scan. Identification runs on-device. Photos are not uploaded to our servers unless you explicitly request community help or report a misidentification.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect precise location data, contacts, calendars, or health data. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service, including identification accuracy and care recommendations;
  • Send you optional care reminders and transactional notifications;
  • Respond to your support requests;
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We will not use your information for targeted advertising, and we will never sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Sharing and disclosure

We share limited information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Service (hosting, crash reporting, email delivery). These providers are contractually limited to using your information only to perform services for us.
  • Payment processors (Apple) for subscription purchases. We receive a transaction receipt but never your full payment details.
  • Legal authorities when required by a valid legal process, and only to the extent necessary to comply.
  • In a business transfer, if Moss is acquired, merged, or sells assets — subject to the same or stronger privacy commitments.

4. Storage and retention

Plant photos and care logs you save are stored on your device by default. If you enable iCloud sync, they are stored in your personal iCloud account under Apple's privacy terms — we do not have access to that data.

Account and support information is retained for as long as your account is active and for up to 90 days after deletion for backup and fraud-prevention purposes, after which it is permanently deleted.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict the processing of your personal information. You may also object to certain processing and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, open the app (Settings → Data), or email support@mossplant.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Children's privacy

Moss is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. International users

Moss is operated from the United States. If you use the Service from outside the U.S., your information may be transferred to and processed in the U.S. We rely on appropriate safeguards (including Standard Contractual Clauses) when transferring personal data from the European Economic Area, UK, or Switzerland.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+), encryption at rest for server-side data, and strict access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work hard to keep your data safe.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy here and, if you have an account, by email at least 14 days before changes take effect.
